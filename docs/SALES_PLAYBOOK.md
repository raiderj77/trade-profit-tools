# 90-day sales playbook

Status: draft only. This document does not authorize outreach, follow-ups, payment requests, or account changes. Jason must explicitly approve the list, message, mailbox, and start of sending.

## Target

- First $500 deposit within 14 days.
- Five $1,000 customers within 90 days.
- One offer.
- One channel.
- No paid ads.

## Sales assets

- Offer page: `https://calculator.yourfriendlydeveloper.com`
- Live demo: `https://calculator.yourfriendlydeveloper.com/demo`
- Seller brand: Your Friendly Developer

Do not send prospects to a new or unrelated product brand.

## Prospect profile

Look for small U.S. agencies with:

- Clear home-service clients.
- A founder or owner visible on the website.
- A basic contact form but no interactive calculator.
- Services such as PPC, local SEO, websites, call tracking, or lead generation.
- Evidence of work with plumbers, HVAC companies, roofers, electricians, landscapers, or remodelers.

Avoid:

- Large agencies with procurement layers.
- Generalist agencies with no home-service proof.
- Agencies that already sell a mature calculator platform.
- Prospects with no named decision maker.

I put together a short preview for [Agency Name]. Should I send it?

Jason
Your Friendly Developer

Jason

## Daily process

- Research 10 agencies.
- Write one relevant sentence for each.
- Send 10 individual emails.
- Record sent date, response, follow-up date, and outcome.
- Follow up after three business days.
- Follow up once more after five business days.

## Proof gates

Day 7:

- 40 delivered emails.
- At least two interested replies.

Day 14:

- 80 delivered emails.
- Four interested replies or one deposit.

Day 30:

- 150 delivered emails.
- At least one paying customer.

## What to change when a gate fails

Change one variable at a time:

1. Prospect quality.
2. First sentence relevance.
3. Offer wording.
4. Demo clarity.
5. Price presentation.

Do not respond to silence by adding software features.
