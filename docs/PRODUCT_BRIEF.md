# Product brief

## Seller

Your Friendly Developer.

Main domain: `yourfriendlydeveloper.com`.

Application launch domain: `calculator.yourfriendlydeveloper.com`.

## Business structure

This repository contains two connected assets.

### Cash offer

Home-Service Lead Value Calculator.

A custom white-label calculator sold to small U.S. marketing agencies serving home-service businesses.

### Trust and research asset

Opportunity Lab.

A public section that turns source-backed market signals into scored, testable offers. It helps prospects understand how Your Friendly Developer chooses what to build.

The calculator remains the commercial priority during the first 90 days.

## Calculator buyer

Small U.S. marketing agencies focused on home-service businesses.

Primary decision makers:

- Agency owner.
- Founder.
- Head of growth.
- Client strategy director.

Best initial niches:

- Plumbing.
- HVAC.
- Roofing.
- Electrical.
- Landscaping.
- Remodeling.

## Calculator buyer problem

Agency websites often rely on generic contact forms. Prospective contractor clients do not see a personalized financial reason to start a conversation.

Agencies also need sales tools that make lead quality, missed calls, close rates, and average job value easier to discuss.

## Calculator offer

A custom-branded lead-value calculator installed on the agency website within five business days.

Founding package:

- Agency logo, name, colors, and copy.
- Responsive contractor-facing calculator.
- Lead capture after results.
- Email notification to the agency.
- Standalone page or iframe embed.
- Source code.
- One revision.
- Installation assistance.
- No monthly fee.

Price:

- $1,000 total.
- $500 deposit before custom work.
- $500 after installation.

## Core promise

Give contractor prospects a financial reason to contact the agency.

Do not promise more leads, revenue, profit, or sales. The calculator provides an estimate and a stronger conversation starter.

## Calculator user flow

### Agency buyer

1. Opens the Your Friendly Developer sales page.
2. Reviews the offer and live demo.
3. Requests a branded preview or pays the deposit.
4. Sends logo, brand colors, preferred email, and website access details.
5. Receives the installed calculator.

### Contractor prospect

1. Opens the agency-branded calculator.
2. Enters lead and conversion numbers.
3. Sees estimated revenue, missed-call loss, break-even CPL, and improvement potential.
4. Submits contact information to receive or discuss the estimate.
5. The agency receives the data by email.

## Opportunity Lab user flow

1. A visitor opens `/opportunities`.
2. The visitor compares verdicts, score, startup assumption, first-payment assumption, and contact level.
3. The visitor opens a brief.
4. The brief shows evidence, buyer, offer, red flags, and a 72-hour validation test.
5. The visitor requests a small proof build or opens the current calculator offer.

## Commercial proof gates

- Day 7: 40 delivered personal emails and at least two interested replies.
- Day 14: 80 delivered emails and either four interested replies or one deposit.
- Day 30: 150 delivered emails and at least one paying customer.

A failed gate triggers a change to the target list, message, or demo. It does not trigger more product features.

Opportunity Lab earns more investment only after a brief produces a paid build request, a qualified buyer reply, or repeat traffic across several briefs.
