# Decisions

## Accepted

- Seller brand: Your Friendly Developer.
- Main domain: `yourfriendlydeveloper.com`.
- Application launch domain: `calculator.yourfriendlydeveloper.com`.
- No new domain purchase for the MVP.
- Primary product type: productized custom calculator service.
- Primary buyer: small home-service marketing agencies.
- End user: contractor prospects.
- Calculator price: $1,000.
- Deposit: $500.
- Delivery: five business days.
- Proposed acquisition channel after owner approval: small-batch, individually researched business email.
- Framework: Next.js App Router with TypeScript.
- Styling: dependency-free global CSS.
- Hosting: Vercel.
- Email: Resend.
- Storage: none.
- Payments: hosted Stripe Payment Link.
- White-label method: one configuration file per customer deployment.
- Scope strategy: manual service before SaaS.
- Research section: Opportunity Lab.
- Lab purpose: trust, lead generation, and evidence-based product selection.
- Lab content model: manually reviewed static briefs in the repository.
- Lab score: ten dimensions, 100 points total.
- Lab publication rule: agents assist, humans approve.
- Lab source rule: original wording and visible first-party or authoritative sources.

## Deferred

- Sending outreach or follow-ups.
- Requesting deposits or activating a public payment link.
- Final contact email.
- Stripe Payment Link.
- Resend sender verification.
- Final seller logo.
- CRM webhook.
- PDF export.
- Niche-specific calculator versions.
- Merging this project into a route on the main website.
- Optional `lab.yourfriendlydeveloper.com` redirect.
- Consent-based Opportunity Lab email digest.
- Research intake form.
- Read-only agent research pipeline.
- Additional opportunity briefs after source review.

## Rejected for this phase

- A new product domain.
- Authentication.
- Database.
- Multi-tenant architecture.
- Customer dashboard.
- Subscription billing.
- AI chat or automatic idea generation.
- Autonomous publishing.
- Live scraping service.
- Automated agency prospecting.
- Mass cold email.
- Analytics dashboard.
- Paid idea membership.
- Copying another idea newsletter's wording, graphics, or research.
